Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: Chicago doesn't collect any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers of its own. Your settings, stats and saved game stay on your iPhone. If you play with friends, the name you choose and your moves go straight to the other players' phones, either over your local network or through Apple's Game Center. None of it reaches me.

1. Who is responsible

Chicago is made and published by an independent developer, Magnus Cederberg, based in Sweden ("I", "me"). You can reach me at mcederdev@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Chicago app for iPhone and this website.

2. Data I collect

None. Chicago has no sign-in, no account and no servers of its own, and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from me or anyone else. I don't receive your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or anything else from the app, and I never sell or share data, because I don't have any.

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use that only to answer you.

3. Data that stays on your iPhone

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your settings: rules, card style, animation speed, hints, sounds and haptics,
  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later,
  • a record of each finished game for the Stats screen: the date, the names at the table, the final scores, the difficulty, your Chicago calls and your best hand, and
  • for playing with friends: the player name you type and a random ID the app makes up, used to give you your seat back if you drop out of a game.

This data is never sent to me. It is deleted when you delete the app. If you have device backups turned on, Apple may include it in your backup under Apple's own terms.

4. Playing with friends

Playing with other people is optional. Games against computer players never use the network.

Nearby tables (same Wi-Fi)

The first time you host or look for a table, iOS asks whether Chicago may find devices on your local network. If you allow it, the app uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity to show and find tables nearby. To do that it shares, with nearby devices running Chicago:

  • the player name you typed, and your table's name (for example "Magnus's table") if you host, and
  • while you play: your random player ID and your moves in the game.

This goes directly between the phones, encrypted, without passing through any server. Other players at the table see your name. You can turn local network access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, and Chicago still works for games against computer players.

Game Center

If you choose to play through Game Center, you sign in with your Apple Account through Apple's own Game Center screens, and Apple finds players and carries the game between phones. Other players see your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy, and you can manage it in Settings → Game Center. I don't receive your Apple Account details or any data about your matches.

5. App Store and payments

Chicago is downloaded from Apple's App Store, which handles your download under Apple's own terms. Apple sends me sales and download reports that do not identify you. The app has no in-app purchases.

6. Children

Chicago does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. When playing with others, players see only the name each player chooses, so choose one that doesn't reveal who you are if you prefer. If you believe a child has emailed me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your iPhone is kept until you delete the app. Emails to me are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted, at the latest after 24 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to data portability. Since the app sends me no data, you control all of it on your device. For the emails you send me, write to me and I will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

9. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting new kinds of data, I will update this policy before that version is released and mention it in the release notes.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: mcederdev@gmail.com
