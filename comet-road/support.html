Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Use the stick on the left to steer. Push it up to go faster and pull it down to brake. Tap JUMP to jump over gaps and onto blocks, and tap PHASE to switch your ship between blue and orange.

What do the different tiles do?

Grey tiles are normal road. Blue tiles only hold a blue ship and orange tiles only an orange one. Yellow tiles give you a speed boost, purple tiles slow you down, and red tiles are hot and destroy your ship. Tall blocks can be landed on, but flying into their side ends your run.

Can I use a game controller or keyboard?

Yes. Connect any controller that works with iPhone or iPad. The left stick steers, the right trigger speeds up, the left trigger brakes, A jumps, and B or the right shoulder button switches phase. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD, space to jump, and X or Shift to switch phase.

How do I unlock more levels?

Each level unlocks when you finish the one before it. Today's track and unique tracks are always open, no unlocking needed.

When does Today's track change?

At midnight, using the date on your phone. Everyone gets the same track on the same day.

Why am I seeing ads?

Comet Road is free, and ads help pay for it. You only see an ad if you choose to, when the game offers to let you continue after a crash. You can always say no and restart the level instead.

An ad didn't load or I didn't get my reward

Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes there is simply no ad available. Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email me and let me know.

I lost my progress

Your progress is saved only on your device. If you deleted the app or moved to a new phone without restoring a backup, it can't be recovered. Restoring an iCloud or computer backup of your device usually brings it back.

Does the game collect my data?

I don't collect any personal data. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some data to show and measure ads. You can say no to tracking when iOS asks. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap Sound on in the menu to switch it to Sound off. The game also respects your phone's volume.
