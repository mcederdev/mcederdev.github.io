COMET ROAD

Fly a ship down a road of floating tiles through deep space. Steer, jump the gaps, and switch between blue and orange to land on the right tiles. One mistake and you're stardust.

Coming soon to theApp Store Free to play on iPhone

WHAT'S ON THE ROAD

100 LEVELS

Twelve hand-built levels to learn the ropes, then 88 more that get faster, narrower and meaner. Every player gets the same tracks, so times are comparable.

TODAY'S TRACK

A brand new track every day at midnight. Same one for everyone, so you can race your friends for the best time.

UNIQUE TRACKS

Generate a track nobody has flown before. Each one gets its own six-character code, and there are billions of them.

TOUCH OR CONTROLLER

Play with on-screen controls, or connect a game controller or keyboard. No account, no sign-in, no internet needed to play.

SWITCH PHASE TO SURVIVE

Your ship is either blue or orange, and it only lands on tiles of its own color. Grey tiles take anyone.

Flip phase at the right moment, mid-jump if you have to, and the road opens up. Flip too late and you fall straight through.
